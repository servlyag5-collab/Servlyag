# App stores: can merchants pay on the website only? (checked 2026-11-09)

Owner's wish: merchants pay for plans on servlyag.com only (ACB / CyberSource); the iOS and Android apps
just show the plan. This note is Claude's reading of the stores' current rules, not legal advice. Re-check
the live pages before submitting; rules change.

## What the rules say (short quotes)

**Apple, App Store Review Guidelines** (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- 3.1.1: "If you want to unlock features or functionality within your app, (by way of example: subscriptions
  ...), you must use in-app purchase."
- 3.1.1(a): outside the United States storefront, apps "may not include buttons, external links, or other calls
  to action that direct customers to purchasing mechanisms other than in-app purchase."
- 3.1.3(b) Multiplatform services: users may use subscriptions bought on your website "provided those items
  are also available as in-app purchases within the app."
- 3.1.3(g): buying "boosts" to display in the same app "must use in-app purchase" (closest match to paid top
  placement).
- 3.1.3(e) (physical services consumed outside the app) covers customers paying a hairdresser, NOT the
  merchant's Servly plan. 3.1.3(f) (free companion apps) only covers VoIP, cloud storage, email, web hosting.

**Google Play, Payments policy** (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738)
- Section 2: payment "for access to in-app features or services" must use Google Play's billing system;
  examples include "service upgrades" and "business productivity software".
- Section 3 exceptions are physical goods/services (cleaning, transport, ...): a Servly plan is not one.
- Section 4: apps "may not lead users to a payment method other than Google Play's billing system"
  (buttons, links, messaging).
- Alternative-billing programs (user choice billing, billing choice, external offers/payments) list the US,
  EEA, UK, Japan, Australia, South Korea, India, Brazil, Indonesia, South Africa: **not Antigua and Barbuda**.

## What it means for Servly

A Servly plan unlocks things inside the app (listings shown, top placement, the booking calendar). Both
stores treat that as a digital in-app feature. So **website-only payment, with the app reflecting the plan,
is likely to be rejected** in Antigua's storefronts. And today's app has a Plan screen with prices and a Pay
button that opens the website: that is a "call to action" to pay outside the store, which both stores forbid.

## Options (owner decides)

1. **Website + store billing (most likely to pass).** Keep ACB on the website, and ALSO sell the plans in the
   apps through Apple In-App Purchase and Google Play Billing (Apple 3.1.3(b)). Store fees: 15% under Apple's
   Small Business Program and Google's 15% rate for subscriptions (check current rates). The in-app price can
   be a bit higher than the website price. The app never mentions the website price or links to it.
2. **Merchants only on the website.** The store apps are for customers (browse, message, call, book); merchant
   tools (plans, listings, payment) live only on servlyag.com. Lowest cost, but merchants lose the app for
   their side; still some review risk if merchant features appear in the app at all.
3. **Website only, app shows the plan, no links or prices (owner's current wish).** Cheapest, but most likely
   to be rejected under Apple 3.1.1 / 3.1.3(b) and Google Section 2. Could be tried, at the risk of a rejection
   and a resubmission.

Whatever is chosen, before submitting: the iOS/Android apps must not show the Pay button, website prices or a
link to pay on the website (unless option 1 sells the same plan in-app).
