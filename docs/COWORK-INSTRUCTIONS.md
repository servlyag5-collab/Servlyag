## What Servly is

A marketplace app (Android, iPhone, website) where local businesses in Antigua and Barbuda list their
services and customers find them. Customers use it free: search, message, call, and book (where the business
has bookings). Tagline: "Whatever you need, someone's ready."

Businesses pay a monthly plan, by card on the website (Antigua Commercial Bank, using CyberSource):

| Plan | Price (XCD a month) | Listings | Placement in search | Bookings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 50 | 1 | normal | +25 XCD upgrade |
| Growth | 100 | 2 | priority (after Business) | +25 XCD upgrade |
| Business | 170 | 4 | top | included |

- Search shows Business first, then Growth, then Starter; inside each plan, businesses take turns at the
  front every half hour. Never say Servly has "no algorithm".
- Founding Merchants get a free trial (default end date 8 March 2027). Promo code LAUNCH50 (50% off the plan
  for 2 months, 25 places) only works from launch day, when the owner switches it on.
- Bank fees: ACB charges 75 USD a month plus 4% of every payment.
